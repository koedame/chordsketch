# Slide template snapshots

PNG exports (3840×2160, 2× of the 1920×1080 frames) of the ten presentation
slide templates that were kept in `design-system/chordsketch.pen`
(`Slide 01 — Cover` … `Slide 10 — Closing`).

The `.pen` design file has been removed. These images are a record of how the
templates looked when it was removed and are not updated afterwards. The
implementation and `design-system/tokens.css` are the source of truth; the
colors, fonts, and the logo used by the slides are all defined there and in
`assets/logo.svg`, and the icons are from [Lucide](https://lucide.dev).
