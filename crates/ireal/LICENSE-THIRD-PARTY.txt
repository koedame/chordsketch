The iReal Pro URL de-obfuscation in `src/parser.rs` (`unscramble` and `obfusc50`)
is a Rust translation of `unscramble.js` from pianosnake/ireal-reader
(https://github.com/pianosnake/ireal-reader), which in turn follows
`irealb_parser.lua` from ironss/accompaniser
(https://github.com/ironss/accompaniser). Some test URLs under `tests/` are taken
from, or adapted from, the test corpus of pianosnake/ireal-reader. Both projects
